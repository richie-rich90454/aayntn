## No affiliation and no endorsement

- This project is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or supported by** the College Board, the United States of America Computing Olympiad, any school, any teacher, or any of the organizations named above.
- The curriculum follows the _content_ of the AP Computer Science A course because that is the starting point of many readers. It reproduces no examination material, no practice examination, no rubric, and no scoring guideline.
- The catalog of problems links to the original statements. The problem statements and the editorials are the work of their authors and remain the property of their authors. **They are not reproduced in this repository.** The worked solutions in the catalog are written from scratch here.
- The templates and the reference implementations are original work. They were written for this project and are covered by its license.

## The rules of a contest

Contest rules change, and they are set by the organizers of the contest and not by this project. In particular:

- The set of languages that a contest accepts for submission is set by the organizers and changes over time. The listings in this repository cover more languages than a contest may accept. The Pascal and JavaScript listings are **educational reference implementations** and are marked as such wherever they appear.
- The rules about what a competitor may consult during a contest are set by the organizers. They usually permit references covering basic language functionality and prohibit algorithm references, prewritten templates and generative AI.
- **Everything in this repository is preparation material.** Read the official rules of every contest before you enter it, and use this material only in preparation.

The authors of this project do not accept submissions of material that violates the rules of a competition, and the maintainers will remove any such material.

Nothing in this repository should be read as a claim that this material was reviewed, approved, or prepared by the College Board, or that a student who studies here is prepared for an examination in any sense beyond the mathematics itself.
